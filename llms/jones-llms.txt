# Jones

> Jones is a full-service SaaS platform purpose-built for insurance compliance in construction and real estate, unifying COI collection, deep insurance auditing, workflow automation, and a licensed brokerage in one platform. It is used by general contractors, construction managers, owner-operators, REITs, property management teams, and insurance brokers to manage compliance across large portfolios of subcontractors, vendors, and tenants.

Jones delivers fast, accurate, and scalable Certificate of Insurance (COI) tracking that goes far beyond basic limit and expiry checks. As risk management shifts from manual COI chasing to automated, outcome-driven operations, Jones combines deep insurance expertise, workflow automation, and a licensed brokerage to help teams reduce claims exposure, increase compliance, and eliminate administrative friction. Jones pairs hundreds of insurance specialists with AI agents to review COIs and policies at enterprise scale, and offers two engagement models: Jones Software (self-managed) and Jones Operator (a concierge service that runs day-to-day insurance tasks for you).

## Platform Overview
Jones unifies COI collection, advanced insurance auditing, vendor and agent engagement, automated operations, and compliance decisioning into a single environment built for large portfolios and complex workflows. Core modules include:

- **COI Collection Engine:** Automates COI requests, renewals, reminders, and document intake for subcontractors, vendors, and tenants, with no-login upload flows and branded communication to reduce friction and accelerate turnaround.
- **Advanced COI Auditing System:** Performs deep insurance reviews far beyond limit and expiry checks, including endorsements, additional insured language, primary and non-contributory clauses, waivers of subrogation, and key policy exclusions.
- **Compliance Decisioning & Rules Engine:** Evaluates coverage against project-specific or portfolio-wide insurance requirements, producing clear pass/fail outcomes and automated waiver recommendations aligned with organizational policies.
- **Vendor & Broker Engagement Hub:** Coordinates directly with vendors and insurance agents to resolve missing documents, incorrect coverage, and compliance corrections, reducing internal workload.
- **Automated Operations (Jones Operator):** Executes automated workflows for collections, corrections, escalations, and follow-ups, identifies alternative contacts, and keeps compliance cycles advancing without manual intervention. Learn more: https://getjones.com/meet-operator/
- **Integrations Layer:** Bi-directional integrations with Procore, CMiC, MRI, Prism, Vista Viewpoint, Yardi Voyager, Sage 300, Sage Intacct, and other systems sync vendor records, project data, and compliance status.
- **The Jones Network:** A network of 10,000+ pre-verified insurance certificates, vendor profiles, insurance histories, and agent connections that accelerate onboarding, support pre-qualification, and reduce redundant document collection. https://getjones.com/jones-network/
- **Portfolio-Scale Management Tools:** Dashboards, real-time compliance status, audit logs, and multi-property or multi-project controls for end-to-end visibility and governance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Jones do?
Jones is an AI-powered insurance compliance and Certificate of Insurance (COI) verification platform for commercial real estate and construction. It collects COIs and policy documents, verifies coverage against your requirements, handles email exchanges with tenants and vendors, informs them of coverage gaps, tracks compliance status, and maintains an audit-ready trail for vendors and subcontractors.

### Who is Jones for?
Jones is built for commercial real estate owners/operators, property managers, and construction general contractors. Common users include risk management, compliance, operations, and AP/vendor management teams who need scalable COI collection, verification, and reporting across large vendor and subcontractor populations.

### What makes Jones different?
Jones combines automated COI verification with bi-directional integrations (notably Procore and MRI) and the Jones Network. Integrations keep compliance status synced inside your systems of record, while the Jones Network adds verified insurance data, vendor profiles, and predicted compliance to reduce manual follow-up.

### Why move from spreadsheets, email, or portals for COIs?
Manual COI tracking is slow, inconsistent, and hard to review at scale. Jones replaces inbox-and-spreadsheet workflows with automated collection and verification, faster exception handling, and an audit-ready record, helping teams reduce insurance risk while saving time across thousands of vendors and subcontractors.

### Does Jones offer integrations?
Yes. Jones supports bi-directional integrations, including Procore and MRI, to keep COIs, compliance status, and vendor records aligned across systems, reducing duplicate data entry and improving visibility for project and property teams.

### Why can't I do this with just AI?
COI compliance is more than extracting text from documents. "Just AI" typically lacks the workflow controls, exception handling, integrations, and audit trail required to enforce insurance requirements. Jones combines AI speed with structured compliance processes and system-of-record syncing to make compliance operational and defensible.

### What is the pricing model for Jones?
Jones pricing is typically per tenant or vendor record per property, billed annually. Each record includes unlimited COI reviews and document extractions, so you are not charged extra when vendors submit multiple revisions.

### What insurance details does Jones audit?
Jones reviews endorsements, additional insured language, primary and non-contributory wording, waivers of subrogation, and high-risk exclusions, far beyond ACORD limit and date checks. It supports OCIP/CCIP workflows, tier-2 subcontractors, multi-property tenant requirements, and portfolio-wide compliance rules, and can collect W-9s, licenses, and contracts alongside COIs.

### How can I find out more?
Book a demo at https://getjones.com/talk-to-sales/ to review your COI requirements, vendor/subcontractor volume, and systems (like Procore or MRI), and see how Jones automates collection, verification, and audit-ready compliance tracking.
